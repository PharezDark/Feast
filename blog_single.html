Artisanal Pomegranate Infusions & Fresh Botanical Craft Beverages

At Feast, we believe that exceptional dining begins with balanced flavors that stimulate the senses. Pomegranates have long been celebrated for their ruby brilliance and tart sweetness, making them a cornerstone for our mixology and culinary creations.

Our beverage team carefully selects ripe, locally sourced pomegranates to extract pure, unadulterated juice. By pressing fresh arils daily, we preserve rich antioxidants and subtle floral undertones that pair impeccably with our signature grilled dishes and spicy entrees.

To craft our signature mocktail, fresh pomegranate juice is gently shaken with crushed mint leaves, a splash of citrus, and chilled sparkling water. Served over hand-cut ice, this refreshing drink provides a bright, crisp finish to any multi-course dinner at Feast.

"Great beverages elevate dining into an experience. Balancing acidity, natural sweetness, and fresh herbs is key to crafting an extraordinary palate cleanser."

Whether you are pairing your meal with our seasonal craft drinks or enjoying an afternoon glass in our dining room, every sip is designed to embody fresh, natural luxury. Visit us in Abuja to discover how our culinary team brings fresh botanicals and vibrant fruits to life.
